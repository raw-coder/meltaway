Meltaway

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short: Meltaway has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your timer labels never leave your device. If you agree to anonymous statistics, the app sends us usage numbers (like how long your timers were and which look you picked). Say no and nothing is sent. You can change your mind any time in Settings.

Who we are

Meltaway is developed by Raw Coder, Romania. For any question or request about your data, email rawcoder@icloud.com.

What stays on your device

Your running timer, the labels you type ("Homework", "Tidy my room"), your favourite duration, look, colors and all settings are stored only on your device so the app works. They are never sent anywhere and are deleted when you remove the app.

Anonymous statistics (only with your consent)

On first launch the app asks if you want to help with anonymous statistics. Nothing is sent until you tap "Yes". If you agree, the app sends:

WhatExampleWhy

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifier. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell data.

Where the data goes

Statistics are processed on our behalf by PostHog, an analytics service, on servers in the European Union, under a data processing agreement. PostHog technically receives your IP address when data is sent; it is not stored with the statistics and we do not use it to identify you.

Legal basis and retention

Statistics rely on your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and, for storing the install ID on your device, Romanian Law 506/2004 art. 4(5)). Data stored only on your device is strictly necessary for the app to work. Statistics are kept for at most 12 months, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Say no on first launch, or turn statistics off any time in Settings → Anonymous statistics. Turning it off deletes the random install ID from your device and stops all sending.
  • Ask us to see or delete the data sent from your install: email us the install ID shown in Settings.
  • If you are in the EU/EEA, you can complain to your data protection authority; ours is the Romanian ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro). Residents of other regions (for example California or the UK) have similar rights, and we honor them the same way.

Children

Meltaway is suitable for all ages and does not ask for age. If you are under 16, ask a parent before accepting statistics. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If anything changes (for example, if we ever add ads), we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app, and ask again for any new use that needs your consent.

Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or want your data deleted? Email rawcoder@icloud.com with your device model and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings). We usually reply within a few days.

  • The timer doesn't show on my Lock Screen: check Settings → Lock Screen & Dynamic Island in Meltaway, and iPhone Settings → Meltaway → Live Activities.
  • I didn't hear the end: allow notifications for Meltaway in iPhone Settings → Notifications. Sounds follow the silent switch and Focus modes.
  • Add the widget: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and pick Meltaway.
  • Turn off statistics: Settings → Anonymous statistics.
